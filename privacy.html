Last updated: November 19, 2026

CAONAT (“we”, “our”) operates the website caonat.vip and related internal business systems used to manage our own ecommerce stores.

Information we process

We may process business contact information submitted to us (such as name, email, and message content). For advertising and shop operations, we process first-party performance data from platforms we operate (for example TikTok Shop and TikTok ads accounts owned by CAONAT).

How we use information

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not provide TikTok advertising or shop performance data obtained via API to unaffiliated third parties. Service providers who host infrastructure may process data only to provide those services to CAONAT.

Security

We apply access controls so that API credentials and performance data are limited to authorized internal users.
