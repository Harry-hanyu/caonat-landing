About CAONAT

CAONAT started as a US-focused ecommerce label built around useful, well-presented everyday products. Our team manages product selection, store operations, customer experience, and advertising for our own stores.

We sell primarily through TikTok Shop and supporting digital channels. Performance data from our own shop and ads accounts is used internally to improve assortment, creative, and spend decisions.

Own-store retail

We operate CAONAT storefronts. We are not a multi-brand agency marketplace.

Customer-first catalog

Listings, pricing, and fulfillment coordination stay under CAONAT operations.
