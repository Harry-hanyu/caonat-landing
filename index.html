About CAONAT

CAONAT is a US-focused apparel label built around outdoor leisure and everyday menswear. We design and sell our own catalog — from baggy cargo pants to waterproof shells — through our official Amazon storefront and TikTok Shop.

The brand is operated by 瑞安市绥义跨境电子商务有限公司 (Ruian Suiyi Cross-border E-commerce Co., Ltd.). On Amazon, the seller of record is HANLU-ruian.

Our team manages product selection, store operations, customer experience, and advertising for CAONAT-owned storefronts. Performance data from our shops and ads is used internally to improve assortment, creative, and spend decisions.

Own-store retail

We operate CAONAT storefronts on Amazon and TikTok. We are not a multi-brand agency marketplace.

Apparel-first catalog

Listings, pricing, and fulfillment coordination stay under CAONAT operations.
